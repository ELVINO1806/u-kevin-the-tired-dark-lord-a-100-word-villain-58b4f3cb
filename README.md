A fantasy villain monologue in exactly 100 words. The Dark Lord has pulled the bell rope at dusk for three centuries, the dead still rise on cue, and a hero is climbing the stair demanding a duel at dawn — but the rope is frayed, the steward died two centuries ago, and the list of things to replace is long. His tiredness shows in the pauses and deflections, never in a complaint: he simply lets the hero wait on the cold landing, and sits until the bell forgets his name.

Run `python3 main.py` to write the entry to `/output/entry.txt`.
